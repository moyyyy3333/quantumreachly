Questions

Before you paste a URL.

Do you send email without me?

Yes. Once the account is open, a note sends on its own if it names the person, names their company, and uses what your homepage says. You do not approve each one. If the page was unreadable or the note is generic, it is held and you are not charged.

What is the preview I see before paying?

We read your public homepage, pick buyer groups, and write the notes. Sample contacts use the reserved .example domain. Those notes still have to make sense before they send, and a .example address is not a real inbox.

When does the card get charged?

Not at signup. The card is there so usage can be billed after 48 hours if you do not cancel. Cancel is on the dashboard and stops that auto-charge. Credits already issued stay on the account.

What does $30 cover?

Sent emails are $0.03 each, so the starter credits cover about a thousand sends. Held notes are free. A company search or a people search on the account is $1. The homepage preview is free.

What do you read from my site?

The public page at the URL you paste: the title and the description. We do not ask you to connect an inbox, a CRM, or an ad account to see the first draft.
